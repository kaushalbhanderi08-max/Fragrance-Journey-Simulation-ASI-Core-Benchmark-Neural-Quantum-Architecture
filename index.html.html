Fragrance Journey Simulation

A 100-second animation that follows one fragrance from a smell, to an empty bottle, an online order, factory, warehouse and drone delivery, to the final spray. The brain, cells, wearable sensor and the neural networks of every computer and agent react at each step.

0.0 / 100 s

This is a conceptual, educational model. Real neurons do not connect directly to computers, and the effect of a smell differs from person to person. The cell count (~37 trillion) is an estimate and only a few hundred are drawn. The pop-up and all companies are generic demos with no real brands. The wearable-to-computer link is conceptual, and in practice sharing body data with a feed would need the person's consent. The order is only placed when the human taps Order now (MEDIUM control).
